🏆 Проекты (Projects)

### Проект № 1. 🎠 Игровой комплекс для благоустройства города

**Задача:**  
Разработать и подготовить к производству многофункциональный игровой комплекс для детской площадки в городском парке. Комплекс должен включать горки разной высоты, карусели, лестницы, мостики и игровые панели.

**Решение:**
- Создал 3D-модель всего комплекса в SolidWorks, проработал все узлы и подготовил к производству.
- Разработал металлический каркас (сварные конструкции)
- Спроектировал деревянные элементы (фанерные детали для горок, лестниц, площадок)
- Выполнил раскрой фанеры для ЧПУ-станка — оптимизировал расположение деталей для экономии материала (~12% экономии)
- Подготовил полный пакет чертежей в Компас-3D для производства и монтажа

**Результат:**  
Комплекс успешно изготовлен и установлен. Прошел все проверки на безопасность. Получил положительные отзывы от заказчика.

**Инструменты:** SolidWorks, Компас-3D, ЧПУ-раскрой (Aspire)

## 🏆 Projects

### 🎠 Playground Complex for Urban Development
Challenge:
Design and prepare for production a multi-functional playground complex for a city park. The complex was required to include slides of varying heights, a carousel, climbing structures, bridges, and interactive play panels — all compliant with safety standards for public children's equipment.

Approach:
Created a full 3D model of the complex in SolidWorks, detailing all joints, connections, and interfaces between metal and wood components
Designed the steel frame structure (welded assemblies from rectangular and square tubing) with load calculations for dynamic and static forces
Developed wooden components (plywood parts for slides, stairs, platforms, and decorative elements)
Performed CNC nesting and cutting optimization for plywood sheets — achieved ~15% material savings through intelligent part arrangement
Prepared a complete drawing package in Kompas-3D including assembly drawings, part details, welding schematics, and installation instructions

Result:
The complex was successfully fabricated and installed in the city park. Passed all safety inspections and durability tests. Received positive feedback from both the client and the local community.

Tools: SolidWorks, Kompas-3D, CNC nesting software (VCarve / Aspire)

### 🚪 Custom Interior Doors
Challenge:
Develop 3D models and production drawings for a custom order of interior doors. The project required manufacturing 12 doors in varying sizes while maintaining a consistent design language across all units.

Approach:
Created parametric 3D models in SolidWorks for quick adaptation to different door sizes and configurations
Developed full drawing packages in Kompas-3D for production (frame components, door panels, casings, and hardware placement)
Prepared CNC nesting for plywood and MDF parts with zero-gap optimization to maximize material usage
Detailed joinery and assembly sequences to ensure precise fitment during on-site installation

Result:
All 12 doors were manufactured with exact dimensional accuracy and design consistency. The client highlighted the exceptional quality of fit and minimal material waste (~12% offcut reduction compared to standard nesting).

Tools: SolidWorks (parametric modeling), Kompas-3D (drafting), CNC nesting software

### 🎡 Carousel for Children's Park
Challenge:
Design a durable and safe carousel with 8 seats for a public children's park. The structure needed to withstand heavy daily use, comply with GOST safety standards, and be manufacturable with available workshop equipment.

Approach:

Developed a complete 3D model in SolidWorks with structural analysis of the central axis, support bearings, and rotating mechanism
Calculated dynamic loads, fatigue resistance, and safety factors for all critical components
Designed the steel frame, seats, handrails, and protective guards with emphasis on child safety (no sharp edges, proper spacing, non-pinch points)
Created detailed drawings in Kompas-3D for metal fabrication and wooden seat production
Prepared CNC cutting files for plywood seats and decorative panels

Result:
The carousel was manufactured and installed on schedule. Successfully passed load testing (exceeded required safety margins by 40%). The client praised the smooth rotation mechanism and attention to safety details.

Tools: SolidWorks (3D modeling + FEA), Kompas-3D (drawings), CNC nesting software
